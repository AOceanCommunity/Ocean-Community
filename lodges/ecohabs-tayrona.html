Ecohabs Tayrona

Tayrona National Park, Colombia
Needs official link

Bamboo and palm bungalows inside Tayrona National Park, overlooking the Caribbean. Ecohabs Tayrona is one of the six places Ocean Community features in South America for travelers who want a stay that actually gives something back to the ocean.

Why it is on this list

Solar powered with a zero waste policy, staffed mostly by locals, with part of guest income supporting the park's conservation efforts.
