Our story

About Us

Established in 2020, Ocean Community started as a place to share what we love about the ocean and over time we grew into a community built around one belief, that the best thing we can do is help protect the place we love most.

Since 2020 @ocean_community

Since 2020, Ocean Community has grown from a small group of people sharing what we love about the ocean into a shortlist of stays that put real money and effort back into our oceans. This site is where that turns into something practical. Conservation ties we can name, check and stand behind.

How we choose these six

Every property here is picked for a specific, checkable conservation tie. A named reserve, a funded research program, a stated per night contribution. Not a general sustainability statement. Click through to any listing for the full story and a link to the property's own site. Ocean Community does not process bookings.

We're a small team, and we'd rather feature 36 stays we've actually looked into than thousands we haven't. Every property on this list stayed on it because we could trace the claim back to something real. A named reserve, a funded program, a partnership we could look up ourselves. If we can't, it doesn't make the cut. That's the only bar we hold ourselves to and we recheck it as we go rather than setting it once and moving on.
