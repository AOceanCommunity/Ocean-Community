Likuliku Lagoon Resort

Malolo Island, Fiji

Fiji's only overwater bures, on the Mamanuca Islands. Likuliku Lagoon Resort is one of the six places Ocean Community features in Oceania for travelers who want a stay that actually gives something back to the ocean.

Why it is on this list

The waters in front of the resort were declared a marine protected area in 2005 by the local paramount chief. Sea turtle sightings have increased since.

It is one of six stays we feature in Oceania for exactly this reason: a conservation claim that is specific, sourced, and checkable, not a line of marketing copy.

Good to know before you book

You book directly with Likuliku Lagoon Resort on their own site. Ocean Community does not process the booking or take a cut of what you spend. Every property on Ocean Community is chosen for a named, checkable conservation tie rather than a general green claim, and we recheck that story as we go rather than setting it once and forgetting it.
