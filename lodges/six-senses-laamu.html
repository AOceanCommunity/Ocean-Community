Six Senses Laamu

Laamu Atoll, Maldives

Overwater resort built around a purpose built marine research center. Six Senses Laamu is one of the six places Ocean Community features in Asia for travelers who want a stay that actually gives something back to the ocean.

Why it is on this list

Runs the Maldives Underwater Initiative, a team of ten plus marine biologists that released 3 million coral larvae onto damaged reefs after the 2024 bleaching event, working with Manta Trust, Blue Marine Foundation, and the Olive Ridley Project.

It is one of six stays we feature in Asia for exactly this reason: a conservation claim that is specific, sourced, and checkable, not a line of marketing copy.

Good to know before you book

You book directly with Six Senses Laamu on their own site — Ocean Community does not process the booking or take a cut of what you spend. Every property on Ocean Community is chosen for a named, checkable conservation tie rather than a general green claim, and we recheck that story as we go rather than setting it once and forgetting it.
