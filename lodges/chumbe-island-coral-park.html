Africa · Eco Lodge Spotlight

Chumbe Island Coral Park

Zanzibar, Tanzania

Small eco lodge on the island that is also the world's first privately managed marine protected area. Chumbe Island Coral Park is one of the six places Ocean Community features in Africa for travelers who want a stay that actually gives something back to the ocean.

Why it is on this list

Entirely funded through ecotourism revenue. Profits fund marine education for local schools.

It is one of six stays we feature in Africa for exactly this reason: a conservation claim that is specific, sourced, and checkable, not a line of marketing copy.

Good to know before you book

You book directly with Chumbe Island Coral Park on their own site — Ocean Community does not process the booking or take a cut of what you spend. Every property on Ocean Community is chosen for a named, checkable conservation tie rather than a general green claim, and we recheck that story as we go rather than setting it once and forgetting it.
