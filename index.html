Top 6 · every continent

The world's top 6 eco lodges, villas and hotels, by continent.

Ocean Community started as a place to share what we love about the ocean. This page is an extension of that: the six most genuinely conservation minded places to stay on each continent, gathered by a community built around one belief, that the best thing we can do is help protect the place we love most.

Asia

Verified conservation tie

Raja Ampat, Indonesia

A remote private island dive resort deep in southern Raja Ampat, surrounded by some of the richest reefs on Earth.

Self funds and patrols its own 1,220 sq km marine reserve, established 2005. Fish biomass up 250 to 600% inside it.

About Us

Ocean Community started as a place to share what we love about the ocean, and grew into a community built around one belief: the best thing we can do is help protect the place we love most. This page is where that turns into something practical, a shortlist of stays that put real money and effort back into the water.

How we choose these six

Every property here is picked for a specific, checkable conservation tie. A named reserve, a funded research program, a stated per night contribution. Not a general sustainability statement. Click through to any listing for the full story and a link to the property's own site. Ocean Community does not process bookings or take a cut of anything you spend.

Verified conservation tie means we have found a specific, sourced claim we are confident in. Needs official link means the conservation story checks out but we have not yet confirmed the property's own website to link to. Needs more verification means the conservation claim itself still needs a direct conversation with the property before we would stand behind it fully.
