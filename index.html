How we vet operators

15% of every purchase funds conservation. Every time.

Every operator on this site is checked against the same bar as everything else we've put in front of this community — real environmental impact, not a badge on a brochure. That's why 15% of what you pay for any trip listed here is directed straight into that trip's named conservation project.

  • 15% of every booking's value goes to that trip's named conservation partner — not a general fund
  • A named conservation partner or research tie for every trip, not a general "we care" statement
  • Direct contact with the operator before listing — no third-party resellers
  • Small group sizes and low-impact logistics wherever the destination allows it
