7Pines Resort Ibiza

Ibiza, Spain
Verified conservation tie

Clifftop resort overlooking the Mediterranean on Ibiza's west coast. 7Pines Resort Ibiza is one of the six places Ocean Community features in Europe for travelers who want a stay that actually gives something back to the ocean.

Why it is on this list

Donates to Our Dolphins, a project led by marine research group Tursiops Association studying and protecting Ibiza's resident dolphin population.

It is one of six stays we feature in Europe for exactly this reason: a conservation claim that is specific, sourced, and checkable, not a line of marketing copy.
