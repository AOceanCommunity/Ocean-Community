Raja Ampat, the Reef Misool Eco Resort Calls Home

Ocean Community·5 min read

Misool Eco Resort gets one line in our listing, self funds and patrols its own 1,220 square kilometer marine reserve. That line does a lot of quiet work. Here is the water it is actually talking about.

The heart of the Coral Triangle

Raja Ampat sits off the northwest tip of New Guinea in Indonesia's West Papua province and is usually described as the heart of the Coral Triangle, the stretch of ocean between Indonesia, the Philippines and Papua New Guinea that holds the highest reef biodiversity on the planet. More than 1,500 small islands and islets make up the archipelago, most of them limestone karst formations rising straight out of the water, with reef running right up to their edges.

Why the numbers here are different

Marine surveys have recorded well over 1,300 reef fish species and around 600 coral species in Raja Ampat waters, a fraction of the world's ocean holding a wildly disproportionate share of its reef life. That density is exactly why a self patrolled reserve here matters more than the same reserve would somewhere with thinner biodiversity to protect.

Misool's own reserve, established in 2005, has seen fish biomass rise 250 to 600 percent inside its boundaries. In a region already this rich, that kind of recovery is a sign the patrolling is real rather than symbolic.

A private reserve in public waters

What makes Misool's situation unusual is that the reserve is not government run. The resort funds its own ranger patrols and no take zones across 1,220 square kilometers of the surrounding sea, an arrangement that only works because the resort has stayed in place long enough and committed enough resources to make the patrols mean something. It is one of the clearer examples on this list of a property's own survival being tied directly to the reef staying intact.

What this means if you go

Diving or snorkeling here puts you inside one of the most biodiverse reef systems that has been measured anywhere, protected by patrols the resort pays for itself. Read the full listing for Misool Eco Resort for the booking details and a link to their own site.
