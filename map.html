Asia

Misool Eco Resort

Raja Ampat, Indonesia

A remote private island dive resort deep in southern Raja Ampat, surrounded by some of the richest reefs on Earth.

Self funds and patrols its own 1,220 sq km marine reserve, established 2005. Fish biomass up 250 to 600% inside it.

Six Senses Laamu

Overwater resort built around a purpose built marine research center.
