Africa · Eco Lodge Spotlight

The Zanzibari

Nungwi, Zanzibar, Tanzania

Boutique beach hotel on Zanzibar's northern tip. The Zanzibari is one of the six places Ocean Community features in Africa for travelers who want a stay that actually gives something back to the ocean.

Why it is on this list

Limits plastic use, conserves energy and supports local coral protection efforts.

Every stay on this list is chosen for exactly this kind of claim: specific, sourced and checkable, not a line of marketing copy.

Good to know before you book

You book directly with The Zanzibari on their own site. Ocean Community does not process the booking or take a cut of what you spend. Every property on Ocean Community is chosen for a named, checkable conservation tie rather than a general green claim and we recheck that story as we go rather than setting it once and forgetting it.
