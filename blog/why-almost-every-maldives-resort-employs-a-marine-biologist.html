Why Almost Every Maldives Resort Employs a Marine Biologist

Ocean Community·5 min read

Walk into the average Maldives resort and there is a decent chance one member of staff has a marine biology degree and a job title like "resident marine biologist." It is one of the more distinctive staffing choices in resort tourism anywhere, and it did not happen by accident.

A reef economy with no margin for error

The Maldives is a nation built almost entirely on coral, low lying atolls with barely any land above sea level and an economy that depends on the reef staying healthy enough to keep drawing divers. After the country's reefs were hit hard by the global bleaching event running from 2023 into 2025, that dependency became harder to ignore.

What a resident marine biologist actually does

The job is rarely just guest lectures. It typically covers running coral propagation frames or ropes, tracking manta and whale shark sightings for regional identification databases, testing water quality and briefing dive staff on reef conditions day to day. Some of that work turns into published research; most of it is quieter, ongoing monitoring that would not happen without someone whose job is specifically to do it.

Gili Lankanfushi has kept marine biologists on staff since 2012 and holds EarthCheck Gold Certification, one of only three such certifications anywhere in the Maldives.

Gili Lankanfushi's program

Gili Lankanfushi, in North Male Atoll, has run a rope based coral restoration project alongside its marine biology staff since 2012. The property holds EarthCheck Gold Certification, a sustainability standard only two other resorts in the entire country have reached, which is a higher bar than a marine biologist title alone.

Gili Lankanfushi's place on this list

Gili Lankanfushi is one of the properties we feature in Asia specifically for this combination: staff whose full time job is the reef, a restoration project with a decade of history, and third party certification rather than a claim made only on the resort's own site.
