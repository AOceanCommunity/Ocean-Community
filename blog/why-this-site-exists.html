About us

Why This Site Exists

Ocean Community·4 min read

This started because a few of us were tired of the word eco getting stuck in front of every hotel on earth without meaning anything. So we made a list where it has to mean something. That's really the whole story, but here's the longer version.

It started as a scroll through our own photos

We were the kind of people who plan a whole trip around a reef or a chance to see whales up close and we kept noticing the same thing. A hotel would call itself sustainable, then when you actually looked into what that meant, it was a towel reuse card and a page about "respecting nature." Nothing you could check. Nothing that would still be true next year. We started keeping a private list of the places that were different, the ones with an actual reserve attached to them or a named research partner or a number they were willing to put in writing. At some point that list got long enough that it felt wrong to keep it to ourselves.

Why 36 and not 360

Six stays per continent was never a marketing number. It's a limit we set because we're a small group and there's a ceiling on how many properties we can actually look into properly in a year. We'd rather have 36 we've checked than a thousand we've skimmed. If the list grows slowly, that's the reason, not a lack of ambition.

What matters to us is not how a listing came to our attention. It's whether the conservation claim behind it actually holds up when we look into it. That's the part of this we're not willing to loosen, however the site grows.

What we get out of it

Honestly, mostly the same thing you might get out of reading it. An excuse to keep digging into places doing this well and a reason to stay a little bit more informed than the average traveler about which conservation claims are real. We post the good finds on Instagram as we go, which is also where you'll hear about new additions before they make it onto the site properly.

The part that won't change

Sites evolve. Ours might too, in ways we can't fully predict yet. What we're not willing to compromise on is the bar itself. A claim has to be specific and it has to be checkable, or it doesn't get a spot. That's the one rule this whole project was built around. It's the reason we'd rather keep this small than grow it fast.
