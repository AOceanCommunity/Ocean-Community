Why We Don't Take a Cut of Your Booking

Ocean Community·4 min read

Every listing on this site ends the same way: a link out to the property's own site, and a line reminding you that Ocean Community doesn't process the booking or take a commission. That's easy to skim past, so here's why it's actually the point.

Most travel sites don't work this way

The usual model for a travel or booking site is a commission on every reservation, sometimes 15 to 25% of the room rate. That's a fine business model, but it creates a quiet incentive: list whatever converts, feature whoever pays for placement, and let "sustainability" become a marketing filter rather than a real bar. We didn't want that incentive anywhere near this list.

No revenue means no reason to loosen the bar

Ocean Community doesn't make money from which 36 properties are on this site or how many people click through to them. That's not a virtue we're claiming credit for, it's a structural choice: if we can't trace a conservation claim back to something checkable, there's no commission on the line to make us look the other way. The vetting process described in how we vet a conservation claim only works because nothing here depends on a booking going through.

You book directly with the property, on their own site, at their own rate. We don't see a cent of it either way.

What it costs us

The honest tradeoff is that Ocean Community doesn't have a built in revenue stream. It runs as a showcase, not a business, which means the six person continent lists and the vetting behind them are done because a small team decided it was worth doing, not because it's funded by a cut of anyone's stay. We're fine with that tradeoff. It's the only way this list stays honest about what it's recommending.

What it means for you

When you click "Visit official site" on a listing, you're leaving Ocean Community entirely. No affiliate tracking, no referral code, no markup baked into the rate you're shown. If a property is on this list, it's because the conservation tie checked out on its own terms, not because it paid to be here or because we get paid when you book it.
