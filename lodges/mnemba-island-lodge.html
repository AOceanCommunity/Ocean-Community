Mnemba Island Lodge

Zanzibar, Tanzania
Needs official link

Exclusive small lodge inside the Mnemba Island Marine Conservation Area, run by andBeyond. Mnemba Island Lodge is one of the six places Ocean Community features in Africa for travelers who want a stay that actually gives something back to the ocean.

Why it is on this list

Tied to a funded coral nursery and reef restoration research program running 2024 to 2027 with three universities.
