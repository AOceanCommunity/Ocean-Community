North America · Eco Lodge Spotlight

Aguila de Osa

Drake Bay, Costa Rica

Rainforest and marine adventure lodge overlooking Drake Bay. Aguila de Osa is one of the six places Ocean Community features in North America for travelers who want a stay that actually gives something back to the ocean.

Why it is on this list

Gives direct access to the Caño Island Biological Marine Reserve, one of Costa Rica's most tightly protected dive sites.

It is one of six stays we feature in North America for exactly this reason: a conservation claim that is specific, sourced, and checkable, not a line of marketing copy.

Good to know before you book

You book directly with Aguila de Osa on their own site — Ocean Community does not process the booking or take a cut of what you spend. Every property on Ocean Community is chosen for a named, checkable conservation tie rather than a general green claim, and we recheck that story as we go rather than setting it once and forgetting it.
