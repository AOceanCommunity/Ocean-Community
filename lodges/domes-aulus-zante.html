Domes Aulus Zante

Laganas Bay, Zakynthos, Greece

Resort on the beach fronting Laganas Bay, inside the National Marine Park of Zakynthos. Domes Aulus Zante is one of the six places Ocean Community features in Europe for travelers who want a stay that actually gives something back to the ocean.

Why it is on this list

Adopted 70 loggerhead turtle nests from the beach in front of the resort in 2023 and another 70 in 2024, in partnership with ARCHELON, and funded the rescue of an injured turtle.

It is one of six stays we feature in Europe for exactly this reason: a conservation claim that is specific, sourced, and checkable, not a line of marketing copy.

Good to know before you book

You book directly with Domes Aulus Zante on their own site. Ocean Community does not process the booking or take a cut of what you spend. Every property on Ocean Community is chosen for a named, checkable conservation tie rather than a general green claim, and we recheck that story as we go rather than setting it once and forgetting it.
